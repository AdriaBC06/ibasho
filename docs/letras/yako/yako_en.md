# Yakō — letra en inglés

## Estrofa 1

Window shows the city lights asleep below  
you and I aboard the midnight train  

Riding waves of neon through the silent sea  
all the way until the dawn  

## Pre-estribillo

Hear the rhythm of the rails singing to me  
and my heart is screaming out loud  

## Estribillo

Flying through the midnight never looking back  
you and I together to the end of time  
high above the heavens we become the wind now  
till we see the morning light  

## Estrofa 2
